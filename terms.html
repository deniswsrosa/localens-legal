What LocaLens is

LocaLens translates text in photos, typed text and live conversation using machine-learning models that run on your device. It is provided free of charge and supported by ads (see the Privacy Policy).

Translations can be wrong

  • Machine translation, text recognition and speech recognition make mistakes: words can be misheard or misread, and meaning, tone or names can be translated incorrectly or left out.
  • Do not rely on LocaLens where a mistake could cause harm — for example medical instructions, dosages, allergy or safety information, legal, financial or official documents, or emergencies. Use a qualified human translator or interpreter for those.
