LocaLens — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 16, 2026

Privacy Policy · Terms of Use

LocaLens ("the app") is an offline translator for photos, typed text and live conversation. Everything you photograph, say or type is processed on your device and stays there unless you choose to send it as feedback — translation, text recognition in photos and speech recognition all run on the phone, and work in flight mode. LocaLens has no account and does not collect your content, apart from feedback you choose to send.

The app uses the internet for three things only: downloading its translation and speech models once; — only if you agree — sending anonymous usage statistics; and — only when you choose to — sending feedback about a translation or the app. All are described below. LocaLens shows no ads and contains no advertising or tracking SDK.

What stays on your device

  • Photos and camera images you translate — read on the device; LocaLens does not upload them.
  • Microphone audio in Conversation and voice input — turned into text on the device while you use the feature; audio is not recorded to storage or sent anywhere, unless you turn on the record button (off by default) and then choose to share the recording (see "Feedback you choose to send").
  • Text you type, paste or share into the app, and all translations — produced and kept on the device.
  • Your settings (languages, voice speed, earbuds mode and similar) — stored in the app's private storage.

Uninstalling the app deletes all of it, including the downloaded models.

Model downloads

Translation, speech and text-recognition models are downloaded when you choose them (for example in the setup wizard or the Settings tab). They come from public model hosts (Hugging Face and GitHub) and from LocaLens' own download server (hosted on Cloudflare). Like any web server, these services receive technical connection data such as your IP address and device type while a download runs; LocaLens sends them nothing about you or your content, and our own server does not store IP addresses.

Optional anonymous usage statistics

Off unless you agree. The app may ask once whether you want to share anonymous usage statistics to help improve LocaLens; you can change your answer at any time in the app's settings. If you agree, the app sends one small summary per day to LocaLens' own server (Cloudflare), containing:

  • Languages: your phone's language and the app's display language; which language pairs you translate between, per feature, with counts; languages you add or remove.
  • Feature use: how often you use the camera, typed text, conversation, earbuds mode and voice playback; conversation time in broad ranges; whether you finished the setup and tours.
  • Quality and speed: signs that a result needed correcting (for example switching the detected language); how long models take to load and translate; errors and crashes (the error type and the part of the app involved, never text).
  • Downloads: which models were downloaded or failed, and free storage in broad ranges.
  • Device: app version, Android version, device model, memory size range.
  • A random statistics ID created on your device (not your advertising ID or any other device ID), so days of use can be counted. You can reset it in settings.

The statistics never include your photos, audio, recognized speech, typed text or translations, and are not linked to your name, email or advertising ID. Our server does not store IP addresses. Daily summaries are kept for up to 24 months, then deleted.

Feedback you choose to send (optional)

"Didn't understand". During a live conversation each half of the screen has a "Didn't understand" button, in that half's language. Tapping it asks the speaker, on their half, to say it another way, and marks that line on your phone. A mark by itself is never sent.

Sending the marked lines (off unless you agree). The app may ask once — together with the statistics question — whether to send the lines you mark; you can change this at any time in Settings › Privacy. If it is on, or if you choose "Send" after a conversation, the app sends, for each marked line only: the words that were recognised and the translation exactly as it was shown (and the corrected translation if it changed a moment later), the line just before it, for context, the two languages, the app version and the names of the speech and translation models used.

Sharing a whole conversation (only when you choose it). After a conversation you can rate it and, if something went wrong, choose to share the whole conversation as text. If you also recorded the conversation (the record button, off by default), you can include the recording — only if everyone in it agreed. Recordings are sent only over Wi-Fi.

Messages you type. Settings › About › "Send feedback / Report a problem" lets you write us a message; after a problem the app may also offer it ("Something went wrong? Tell us"), at most once a day. Only the text you type is sent, with the app version — nothing else.

Before anything is stored for sending, numbers of 4 or more digits and e-mail addresses are removed on your phone. A marked line may contain the words of the person you were talking to; please don't use this for conversations with private details, and don't type personal details into a message.

Ratings. If anonymous statistics are on, a thumbs up or down after a conversation is sent with: the day, the language pair, the app version, the reasons you picked, how many lines were translated and which of them (by number) you marked. No text.

Anonymous. None of this carries an account, your name, e-mail, phone number, advertising ID or any device ID, and our server does not store IP addresses. Each shared item gets a random reference made on your phone. To stop abuse, each request carries a short-lived token from Google Play Integrity (it confirms a genuine copy of the app; it does not identify you to us).

Offline first. What you send waits on your phone until it is online (recordings: until Wi-Fi), for up to 30 days (ratings: 14 days); then it is deleted unsent.

Use. We use feedback to find and fix bad translations and recognition: to build test sets and to improve and train LocaLens' translation and speech models. It is not sold or shared with anyone.

Keeping and deleting. Shared lines, conversations, recordings and messages are deleted from our server automatically after 6 months; ratings after 24 months. Settings › Privacy › "Shared with LocaLens" lists what you shared, with a Delete button for each item: it removes it from our server (or drops it from your phone if it was not sent yet).

Rating the app on Google Play. After a few successful translations the app may show Google Play's own rating card (at most once every 30 days). Google handles that card; LocaLens does not see whether or how you rated.

Permissions and why they're used

  • Camera — to photograph text you want translated. Images are processed on the device.
  • Microphone — for Conversation and voice input. Speech is recognized on the device while the feature is on.
  • Internet — to download models and, if you agree or choose to, send anonymous statistics and feedback.
  • Notifications, foreground service (data sync), wake lock — to show download progress and keep large model downloads and live conversations running reliably.

Data sharing

LocaLens does not sell or share your content with anyone — it only ever has the feedback you choose to send. The only third parties involved are the model hosts named above (to deliver downloads) and Cloudflare (which runs LocaLens' download, statistics and feedback server).

Children

LocaLens is a general-purpose translation tool and is not directed at children under 13.
